About Pamela

As the cultural attaché to the King of Spain, Pamela learned how to provide clients with truly royal treatment. A seasoned world traveler who has visited 57 countries, she brings a global perspective and a unique understanding of both international and domestic clientele.

With more than 26 years of real estate experience, Pamela advises buyers, sellers, and investors throughout South Florida's most desirable markets. Her awareness of global economic trends allows her to identify meaningful opportunities in both residential and commercial real estate.

She possesses extensive knowledge of luxury developments around the world, including more than 150 projects designed by Pritzker Architecture Prize–winning architects.

One of her recent highlights includes participation in a record-setting $18.5 million sale at the Four Seasons Surf Club Residences.

Her approach is grounded in trust, discretion, strategic thinking, and highly personalized service.

A Legacy of Excellence

Pamela's commitment to excellence is rooted in an extraordinary family legacy.

Her great-grandfather was appointed U.S. District Attorney in 1905 by President Theodore Roosevelt. Her mother graduated from both Vassar and Harvard, and her father represented Mexico as an Olympic swimmer.

This heritage inspires the discipline, professionalism, and commitment she brings to every client relationship.
